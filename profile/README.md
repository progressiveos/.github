# Progressive OS

**Software built for people and AI agents to work together.**

Progressive OS develops software for content management, commerce, and digital infrastructure.

We believe AI agents should be able to do real work — not just generate suggestions — while people retain control over their systems, data, and publishing decisions.

## What we're building

### [Progressive CMS](https://p-cms.com)

**An agent-native content management system.**

Progressive CMS brings traditional content management and AI-driven workflows into a single platform.

- **Agent-native architecture** — structured interfaces for AI agents to work with content and website operations.
- **Human control** — permissions, review workflows, and explicit publishing decisions.
- **Complete website lifecycle** — content models, themes, media, SEO, publishing, and updates.
- **Self-hosted or managed** — deployment options without locking content to a proprietary hosting platform.
- **Extensible by design** — a modular architecture with versioned extensions and integration contracts.

[Explore Progressive CMS →](https://p-cms.com)

### Progressive Shop

An e-commerce platform being developed around the Progressive CMS ecosystem.

The project is in active development.

## Our engineering principles

- **Ownership matters.** Your content, infrastructure, and data should remain under your control.
- **Agents need boundaries.** Automation should operate through explicit permissions and auditable interfaces.
- **Security is architectural.** Trust boundaries and predictable behavior matter more than feature counts.
- **Extensibility without chaos.** Stable contracts and versioned extensions over unrestricted runtime execution.
- **Open development.** We are preparing selected projects for public source releases and community contributions.
